Vigilance Awareness Week (VAW) - 2023
Dear Students and Teachers
This is to inform you that our school is celebrating Vigilance Awareness Week (VAW) from 31st October to 5th November 2023. The theme for this year's VAW is "Corruption-Free India for a Developed Nation". VAW is an annual observance to promote transparency, accountability, and ethical behaviour in public life. It is also an opportunity to raise awareness about the ill effects of corruption and to encourage people to come forward and report any corruption that they may encounter. During VAW, our school will be organizing a variety of activities to generate awareness about corruption and its prevention. These activities will include: Class Quiz, Slogan, Speech etc. Students of classes X to XII and staff are also encouraged to take the e-pledge, by visiting the website of CVC (https://www.cvc.gov.in )
Regards
