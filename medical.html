MEDICAL ROOM

At HMSSS, the health and care of our children is our prime concern. The school has a well-equipped Medical Room headed by an experienced and qualified MBBS doctor who is assisted by one trained nurse. The team handles any medical emergencies and provides prompt assistance during any emergency that arises during the school hours. In addition, frequent health checkups and workshops are conducted by this department to inculcate the importance of hygiene and health. The team also maintains the health records of all students on roll.
