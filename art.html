Art room

Its time to paint the world with different colours. The Art room gives the budding artists the canvas and platform to make a beginning at the masterpiece they aspire to create one day. They are guided by art teachers to draw, sketch or colour with precision using different mediums. Students seek, strive and pour out their emotions and ideas in their creations. It satiates their desire to create one which has one of the deepest yearnings of the human soul. On the walls are beautiful pictures and paintings that encourage and inspire students to create more such paintings. “The world of reality has its limits but the world of imagination is boundless”
