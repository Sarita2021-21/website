International Day of Persons with Disabilities 2023

Celebrated International Day of Persons with Disabilities 2023 by H.M. Sr. Sec. School

Celebrating the vast spectrum of disability and the limitless of possibilities that we bring forward when we champion accessibility and inclusion. The International Day was commenmorated with a series of impactful activities aimed at raising awareness, fostering inclusivity and providing valuable learning experiences.

Activity 1: Simulation exercise

The activity aimed to : raise awareness about the challenges faced by individuals with disabilities and foster empathy and understanding among participants.

Activity 2: Walk-a-thon

It aimed to raise awareness among students about the challenges faced by individuals with disabilities and the importance of inclusivity. The activity sought to foster a sense of community spirit, emphasizing the collective responsibility for creating an inclusive environment.

Activity 3: PwD Champion Visit

The activity aimed to : provide a personal perspective on living with a disability and inspire others by showcasing the achievements of Ms. Kiran, a psychology student with visual impairment.
