curricular

Success in extracurriculars is a reflection of a thriving school spirit.

Extracurricular activities provide students with a fantastic opportunity to explore their interests, acquire new skills, and build lasting friendships while preparing for their future. At H M Senior Secondary School, we're proud to offer a diverse range of extracurricular options for students of all ages and interests.
Our offerings include:
Sports: From archery to basketball, football, and cricket, our sports teams offer exciting avenues for physical and personal growth.Under the skilled guidance by the coaches,students have bagged prizes not only at state level but also at national level.
Clubs: Our school offers a variety of clubs, encompassing academics, arts and crafts, social engagement, and scientific exploration.
Arts and Crafts: Students can dive into the world of artistic expression with classes in pottery, painting, and drawing.The club instills creative expression, allows students to discover new crafts and creates artwork around the campus.
Volunteer Opportunities: Our school volunteering programme aims to instill the values of service and compassion in every child through activities where children can volunteer. The volunteering is aligned to the cause that the child feels strongly about as well as something that she/he can do easily, either independently or at school with others. We encourage social responsibility with opportunities such as NSS and SPC involvement.

As Prince, a senior in the XII Commerce stream, aptly states, 'Participating in extracurricular activities allows me to explore my passions, develop crucial skills, and build meaningful connections. These experiences have also been invaluable in preparing for my college and future career.'

Lipsita, one of our meritorious students, embodies the numerous advantages of engaging in extracurricular activities.She further quotes the philosophy of Winston Churchill "The pessimist sees difficulty in every opportunity. The optimist sees opportunity in every difficulty." — We are immensely proud to have her as a part of our H M Senior Secondary School community.
