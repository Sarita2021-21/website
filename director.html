Director/Chairman's Message

With great pleasure, I am glad to welcome you to Happy Model Senior Secondary School.
Our fundamental objective is to provide educational excellence, so that each student can make a positive difference during their time with us. In the expectation of excellence, we ensure that children enjoy school in a happy, safe and secure environment designed to fulfil their potential. We also strive to provide students with a caring, supportive and challenging environment in which they can grow and reach respectable heights.
The curriculum of our school has been carefully designed to provide quality education to the children. To meet the needs of high standard education, we incorporate best and innovative practices of education and technology together. We also learned about the role of teachers in the welfare and progress of children. At HMSSS, we have recruited highly qualified, intrinsically motivated, energetic professionals who ensure the development of the child academically and personally and strengthen our vision and mission. Happy Model Senior Secondary School offers an exceptional opportunity in both scholastic and co-scholastic fields. We have a wide range of co-scholastic activities that encourage our children to develop their talents.
Our constant endeavor is to create an environment where education encompassing academics, sports, life skills, innovation and research is given ample scope. The time your child invests in this institute will help them emerge as a mature, successful individual and inspire them to achieve the impossible. I convey my heartfelt appreciation to the efficient and dedicated team of teachers and other staff members for their commitment to keep the torch of the institute high.

The whole purpose of Education is to turn mirrors into windows – Sydney J.Harris.

Gaurav Arora ( Director)
