SST lab

SST lab is a shining example of our dedication to fostering curiosity, innovation, and scientific inquiry among our students.
Our SST Lab is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and a wide range of tools and equipment to support a comprehensive science and technology curriculum. Some of the key features of our lab include: Laboratory Workstations: Spacious and well-equipped workstations for students to conduct experiments in biology, chemistry, and physics.
Cutting-edge Technology: Access to modern computing equipment, 3D printers, and robotics kits that encourage technological exploration.
Safety Measures: Strict safety protocols are in place to ensure the well-being of our students during experiments and projects.
