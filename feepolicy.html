fee rules

  • ⦿ Composite Fee is charged for twelve months from April to March. Fee once paid will not be refunded.
  • ⦿ Composite Fee is accepted at the school fee counter during school hours up to 15th of every month without late fee. Composite Fee is accepted with a late fee of Rs.20 per day till the last day of the month. Student’s name is struck off if the fee is not paid till the last day of the month. Readmission can be sought by paying Readmission Fee of Rs 6000=00 plus late fee as applicable. Readmission is subject to approval by the Principal and management. In case of Cheque dishonour as per the RBI banking norms; Rs. 500 will be charged.
  • ⦿ Admission fee and Caution Money have to be paid once at the time of admission. Caution Money is refundable at the time of leaving the school, on producing the original fee receipt.
  • ⦿ Composite Fee for the months of May and June has to be paid by 15th May. Composite Fee for February and March has to be paid by 15th February. Fee defaulters shall not be allowed to appear for Cycle Test, Terminal Test and Pre-Board & Board Examination.
  • ⦿ Fee receipt will be generated only on producing Fee Card. In case of loss of this card, a duplicate Fee Card will be issued on payment of Rs 100/-.
  • ⦿ Our school does not charge even a single paisa without a printed receipt. Please do not pay any money to any member of staff without a notice from the school. Please ask for a receipt for the money that you pay. Note: If you pay by debit card or credit card then please add 2% to the amount. No additional charges will be levied in case of UPI, NEFT, IMPS, NEFT and Net Banking.
  • ⦿ One month notice or one-month Composite Fee in lieu thereof has to be given if a child is withdrawn from the school. The same rule applies to transport charges. No Child can leave the transport after the August month.
