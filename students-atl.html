HM Innovators

Creativity is thinking up new things. Innovation is doing new things.

Embracing innovation in education promotes critical thinking, a sense of adventure, and an openness to adapt that will serve our students in the classroom. It will provide them with the necessary tools to tackle the challenges of their future workplace and give them the confidence and skills to continue to adapt. Here in our Innovative lab(Atal Tinkering Lab) we develop students ability to innovate, imagine, and create new things which is essential to both academic achievement and general personal growth. In addition, they cultivate analytical thinking, skills for problem-solving, and the ability to learn new things throughout life. Here are the glimpses of our Innovations.
